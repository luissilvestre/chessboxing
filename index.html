How it works Get the game

The idea

Chess practice kids actually ask for

Plenty of kids love chess and want to get better. The trouble is that studying it is slow, and most of them will not sit through much of it. Adults know the feeling.

Chessboxing fixes that by hiding the studying inside a game they want to play. Every chess lesson earns the points you need to win a boxing match, so the next lesson always has a reason.

Training is how you win fights. Not luck, not reflexes.
  1. 1

    Fight

    Step into the ring against a very strange opponent. Jab, cross, block, and try not to get knocked out.

  2. 2

    Train

    Back in the gym, open the chess board. Solve puzzles from your lessons. Clean solves earn stars, and every three stars is a training point.

  3. 3

    Power up

    Spend your points on stamina, strength and speed. Then go back and beat the boxer who stopped you last time.

Winning a fight does not make you stronger. Only studying does. That is the whole trick.

How a lesson plays

  • Learn the idea. Every lesson starts with a short explanation, shown under the board. Switch narration on and it reads it out loud.
  • Find the move. Play it on the board with your finger or mouse. Arrows and circles point out what matters.
  • Keep going. A puzzle is a whole line, not one move. Your opponent answers and you continue to the end.
  • Earn stars. A clean solve is three stars. Every wrong turn costs one. Peeking at the solution earns nothing.

Fighting

Simple controls, serious opponents

One round, no bell. It ends when somebody goes down.

Phone and tablet

On-screen buttons: two arrows on the left, Jab, Cross and Block on the right. A tap is buffered, so a punch you press a hair early still comes out.

How the fights work

  • Jab. Quick, cheap and long-reaching. Great for keeping them honest.
  • Cross. Hurts far more, but costs more stamina and you have to step in to land it.
  • Stamina is everything. Punching spends it and blocking drains it. Run low and you punch slower, move slower and hit softer.
  • Read the buttons. A punch you cannot afford turns grey, and a ring around it shows the recovery still to run.

Your three stats

  • Stamina

    A bigger health bar and a deeper stamina pool. You last longer and punch for longer.

  • Strength

    Every punch hurts more.

  • Speed

    Quicker on your feet and harder to hit.

The ladder gets steep

You begin about as raw as the first opponent. The last one maxes out every stat. You will not out-punch him on instinct, so keep studying and keep spending your points before each climb.

Each rung of the ladder is stronger than the last, and they all fight differently: some dodge, some hide behind their gloves, and some simply never stop swinging.
