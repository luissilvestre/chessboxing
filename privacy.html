Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Chessboxing is a boxing game with chess lessons, suitable for children and adults. We built it to collect as little as possible: the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (training points, boxer stats, chosen lessons and settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Network requests

The app connects to the internet only to download chess content, using public services that need no account:

  • lichess.org for chess puzzles, and
  • GitHub (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com) for chess lesson updates.

These requests do not include any personal information from you. As with any internet connection, the services we contact can see your device's IP address; their own privacy policies apply to that. The puzzle screen has an optional “Analysis” link that opens the puzzle on lichess.org in your web browser. It is off by default (see Children below).

Voice

Spoken instructions use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Nothing is recorded, and nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing to share with or collect from children. The “Analysis” link, which leaves the game for lichess.org, is hidden by default. A grown-up can turn it on in Settings by answering a written arithmetic question, and can turn it off again at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
